## Working title

Call me the hunter (The turtle game)

## The pitch

looks like a retro nintendo gameboy - joystick + buttons. joystick controls the hunter who chases the turtles

## Core loop

a 2d window with a character that attempts to catch the turtles that are actively avoiding him he should have a net over his shoulder and when the player clicks a button the character attempts to catch the turtle in his net. Every so often at random intervals an animation should run that shows the beginning of an earthquake, the player has a fixed number of seconds to leave the area before he is engulfed in the chasm that forms. One the chasm has formed an ad rotates out of the chasm, and then is read out loud.
Add some bad game music in the background as close to seven nation army as
without copyright breach, ensure the music is in an 8 bit style.

## Controls

so looks like old nintendo style gameboy - looks enough different to avoid copyright.

Just the joystick on the right, plus 2 buttons on the left:
button 1 - swing the net to catch the turtle
button 2 - does an emergency jump in a random direction.

## Winning, losing, and what losing costs

turtles start at the top of the screen, hunter at the bottom. Once all turtles are caught the level is complete. dying mean the users can restart that level.

You get points per turtle caught. At the end of the game you can spend points to increase movement speed/net size/hat size (this does nothing)

## Difficulty curve

As levels go up the:

- number of turtles grows (sensible rate)
- turtle movement speed
- ability to avoid the hunter
- reduce the time between earthquakes starts and chasm opens
- obstacles can appear on the map

## Scoring

10 points per turtle, multi-catch bonus of 50% per an additional turtle.

so 2 turtles = 25 points
3 turtles = 60 points
